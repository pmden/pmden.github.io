US Open04 Sep 2026

Sinner is out and Djokovic went in round one. Alcaraz has won four of his last five majors.

Carlos Alcaraz, playing his first tournament since a wrist injury in April, meets Wu Yibing in the third round on Thursday. With Jannik Sinner absent and Novak Djokovic beaten in round one, the defending champion's record at the last five majors he entered reads four titles and one final.

1 · Today

Carlos Alcaraz dropped the first set before advancing to the third round of the 2026 US Open, his first tournament since the wrist injury that ended his spring, and faces Wu Yibing on Thursday 4 September.

US Open, 2 Sep 2026
2 · The event

What happened

Carlos Alcaraz came through his second-round match after losing the opening set and plays China's Wu Yibing in the third round on Thursday 4 September (US Open, 2 Sep 2026). It is his first event since the right-wrist injury that took him out of Rome and Roland Garros in the spring (ATP Tour, May 2026) and then the grass season, and he is seeded second.

The draw has lost its two other favourites. Jannik Sinner withdrew on 21 August with the right-knee injury that has kept him out since Wimbledon (US Open, 21 Aug 2026), which made Alexander Zverev the top seed. Novak Djokovic, who was in Alcaraz's half, lost in five sets to Mariano Navone in the longest first-round match of his Grand Slam career, his first defeat in the opening round of a major since the 2006 Australian Open (Sky Sports, 31 Aug 2026).

Alcaraz arrived in New York with six major titles, the last of them the 2026 Australian Open, where he beat Djokovic in four sets to complete the career Grand Slam and hand Djokovic his first defeat in eleven finals in Melbourne (Sky Sports, 1 Feb 2026) (ATP Tour, 31 Jan 2026).

4 of 5
In 4 of the last 5 majors he entered, Alcaraz won the title: Roland Garros and the US Open in 2025 and the Australian Open in 2026, after the quarter-final exit in Melbourne in January 2025; the fifth was a final at Wimbledon. Every one of those finals was against Sinner or Djokovic. Neither is left in this draw, but Alcaraz has not played a competitive match in five months.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is Alcaraz: four titles in five, the two players who beat him in that stretch are out, and his half of the draw has already lost Djokovic. The title market sits at 50% rather than higher because of the five-month gap; he dropped a set in round two and said he plans to take more time away after New York.

The honest counter-case is match fitness across seven best-of-five rounds. Alcaraz's 2024 US Open, the one early exit in his recent record, came in the second round to an unseeded opponent, and Zverev, Shelton and Fils have all won on hard courts this summer while he was not playing. The prediction fails if the wrist is tested by a long match before the second week.
