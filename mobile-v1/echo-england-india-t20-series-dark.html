England cricket04 Jul 2026

Bethell breaks India. Three nights to finish the job.

Jacob Bethell's unbeaten 76 took England to a four-wicket win at Old Trafford and a 1-0 lead in the five-match T20I series against India. History says leads like this do not survive Indian tours of England.

4 min readEcho #116UK
1 · Today

England beat India by four wickets at Old Trafford, chasing 191 with six balls to spare; Jacob Bethell finished unbeaten on 76 from 46.

ESPNcricinfo, 4 Jul 2026
2 · The event

What happened at Old Trafford

India posted 190 for 7, built on Ishan Kishan's 49 and Abhishek Sharma's 43, before Arshdeep Singh's 3 for 40 kept the chase honest. England got home on 191 for 6 with six balls remaining, Jacob Bethell striking an unbeaten 76 from 46 balls to take the match award and put England 1-0 up (ESPNcricinfo, 4 July 2026).

The chase swung on two overs. With England needing 49 from four, Bethell took down Ravi Bishnoi — three times over the leg side in a 17th over worth 29 runs that included two back-foot no-balls — after an earlier 27-run over had already dented India's control (ESPNcricinfo, 4 July 2026). The series opener at Chester-le-Street on 1 July was washed out without a result.

Three T20Is remain in six days — Trent Bridge on 7 July, Bristol on 9 July and Southampton on 11 July — before a three-match ODI series at Edgbaston, Cardiff and Lord's from 14 to 19 July (ECB, 2026). Shreyas Iyer captains India's T20I side; Shubman Gill takes over for the ODIs.

3 · The pattern

India's white-ball visits: the long series flip

England against India in T20 cricket on English soil splits cleanly by series length. England have won the one-off games. Every time the sides have played a multi-match T20I series in England, India have taken it.

2022
India won the three-match T20I series in England 2-1, sealing it with victories at the Rose Bowl and Edgbaston before England's consolation at Trent Bridge.
2018
India won the three-match T20I series 2-1, taking the opener in Manchester and the decider in Bristol.
2014
One-off T20I at Edgbaston: England won by 3 runs.
2011
One-off T20I at Old Trafford: England won by 6 wickets.
2 of 2
India have won both multi-match T20I series played in England (2018 and 2022) — including 2018, when England also won the first completed match. England's wins in the fixture came only in one-off games. The pattern is genuinely split by format length, and this is the first five-match version.

Split 2-2 overall — but India have won both multi-match T20I series in England.

5 · The read

How to read it

The case for England is the position and the depth of the chase. A 1-0 lead in a five-match series means India must win the majority of the remaining games, and England have just run down 191 without a fully firing top order, on the back of Bethell confirming himself as a finisher of Test-match temperament in T20 clothing. Home conditions, home crowds and a rested pace attack across three venues in six days all lean England's way.

The counter-case is the pattern and the margin. Both times India have played a proper T20I series in England they have lost a game and still won the series — the 2018 tour began with an England win too. Old Trafford turned on one over of Bishnoi no-balls, not on structural superiority, and India's batting produced 190 despite two of their senior players missing out. A 57 per cent market on the series treats the 1-0 lead as decisive; the historical record treats it as the point where India usually start playing.
