Pep built a machine. Maresca has to drive it.

Manchester City confirmed Enzo Maresca as Pep Guardiola's successor on 29 June, on a three-year contract. Ten years and 20 trophies is the inheritance.

4 min read

The end of a decade, and a successor who left a job to wait for it

City confirmed Maresca on a three-year deal; the Italian apologised to Chelsea supporters for his mid-season exit and will pay part of the compensation himself under two confidential settlements.

(Sky Sports, 29 Jun 2026)

Pep Guardiola stepped down after ten years at Manchester City with 20 major trophies, the most successful manager in the club's history (Manchester City, May 2026). "Don't ask me the reasons I'm leaving," he said. "There is no reason, but deep inside, I know it's my time." He stays inside the City Football Group as a global ambassador — close enough to be consulted, far enough to cast a shadow (Sky Sports, Jun 2026).

Enzo Maresca was announced on 29 June on a three-year contract (Sky Sports, 29 Jun 2026). The route was extraordinary: he resigned from Chelsea on New Year's Day, 18 months into the job and six months after winning the Club World Cup, having told the club in autumn 2025 that City had approached him about eventually succeeding Guardiola (ESPN, Jan 2026). British media reported the compensation settlement at about £17m (Al Jazeera, 29 Jun 2026).

The squad build has not waited for the dugout. City have already confirmed Zadok Yohanna from AIK for £24.2m, Pascal Struijk from Leeds for £20m, Costinha from Olympiakos for £11m and Rodrigo Rêgo from Benfica for £3m (ESPN, Jul 2026). Maresca's first competitive fixture is the Community Shield against champions Arsenal in Cardiff on 16 August, six days before the league season opens (Premier League, Jun 2026).

The Pattern

English football has repeatedly asked one man to succeed a manager who defined a club for a decade or more. The record of those successions is mostly grim — sackings inside 18 months are the norm, not the exception. But the one clean success in the sample is total, and it happens to be the case Maresca's defenders cite: the insider promoted from within the building.

2024
Liverpool replaced Jürgen Klopp (9 years) with Arne Slot, who won the title in his first season — and was still dismissed after his second.
2018
Arsenal replaced Arsène Wenger (22 years) with Unai Emery: a 5th-place finish, then sacked after 18 months.
2013
Manchester United replaced Sir Alex Ferguson (26 years) with David Moyes, hand-picked by Ferguson: 7th place, sacked inside 10 months.
1974
Liverpool replaced Bill Shankly (15 years) with his assistant Bob Paisley — who won six league titles and three European Cups, out-achieving the legend.
1969
Manchester United replaced Sir Matt Busby (24 years) with Wilf McGuinness, sacked within 18 months as Busby returned to the job.
1 of 5
Of five successors to English managerial reigns of nine years or more, only 1 of 5 survived beyond two full seasons. The exception, Bob Paisley, was the insider promoted from within — the precedent City are consciously leaning on with a former Guardiola assistant.

1 of 5 successors to reigns of 9+ years lasted beyond two full seasons.

The Read

The weighted case for continuity is that Maresca is the Paisley branch of the pattern, not the Moyes branch. He coached City's youth team, sat beside Guardiola as senior assistant during the domestic treble season of 2018-19, and plays a recognisable dialect of the same football. The machine around him — recruitment, sporting direction, four signings already landed — did not leave with Guardiola. City's summer spending has been early and deliberate, and the £100m threshold is within one mid-priced deal of resolving yes.

The honest counter is that the pattern's failures were also insiders and anointed heirs, and they failed anyway. Moyes was hand-picked; McGuinness was promoted from within; both were gone inside 18 months. Maresca's own record includes walking out of Chelsea mid-season after one league win in seven, and the standard he inherits is not a top-four finish but 20 trophies in ten years. Machines built around one man have a history of revealing, quickly, how much of the machine was the man.
