Questions, mostly answered

Common questions about membership.

What's the difference between free and member access?

Free users see every market, every public Echo summary, and can place predictions. Members get the full editorial briefing on each market (not the preview), the complete set of historical parallels (not just the headline), early access 24 hours before public, and detailed portfolio analytics. The free tier is genuinely useful — membership is for people who want depth.

Is membership required to place real-money predictions?

No. Real-money predictions (once Hunch is UKGC-licensed) are open to all verified users, member or not. Membership is about reading depth, not prediction access. Many members never put money in play — they value the analysis and track record.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly memberships can be cancelled at any point and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period. Annual memberships can be cancelled with a pro-rata refund on the unused months. No questions, no friction.

Is there a free trial?

14 days free for both monthly and annual plans. You won't be charged until day 15, and you can cancel any time during the trial without charge.

How is this different from a newspaper subscription?

Newspapers tell you what happened. Hunch lets you predict what will happen — and tracks how often you were right. Membership adds the depth: the historical context behind each prediction, the editorial reasoning, and the tools to measure your own judgement over time.

Are there any group or corporate plans?

Yes, for newsrooms, hedge funds, policy teams, and university departments. Group access starts at 5 seats. Get in touch at members@byhunch.com for pricing.

When does Hunch launch?

The free product opens with the public launch this September. Members get access from launch day. Founding-member rates lock in for as long as you remain subscribed — no price increases for early supporters.
