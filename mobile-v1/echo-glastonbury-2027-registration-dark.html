Glastonbury01 Jul 2026

The gates stay shut. The queue already forms.

Glastonbury is dark this summer for its first fallow year since 2018, yet registration for 2027 has already opened and organisers have confirmed tickets will go on sale in autumn 2026.

4 min readEcho #116UK
1 · Today

Registration for Glastonbury 2027 is open and free, and organisers say booking information for the 2027 Festival will be released in autumn 2026. Ticket pricing has not been announced.

Glastonbury Festival (official), Jul 2026
2 · The event

A fallow year with a live countdown

For the first time since 2018, no festival is happening at Worthy Farm this June. The 2026 fallow year, confirmed by Emily Eavis to rest the land and return the dairy herd to its pasture, leaves a Glastonbury-shaped hole in the British summer. The festival returns on 23 to 27 June 2027, dates the Eavis family announced last autumn (Glastonbury Festival).

The pause has not slowed the commercial engine. Registration for 2027 tickets is open (Glastonbury Festival, official), free but requiring a passport-style photograph, with booking information promised for autumn 2026 — the sale typically lands in early-to-mid November. The exact sale date and the ticket price remain unannounced.

The defining question is scale itself. More than 210,000 people attend each edition, and a two-year gap compounds demand. The 2025 ticket cost £378.50 with its booking fee; Time Out projects the 2027 price will clear £390 for the first time in the festival's history.

3 · The pattern

What happens when Glastonbury takes a year off

Glastonbury's ticket sales are already the fastest-selling in British live music, and the record shows demand tightens rather than loosens after every pause. The relevant history is sellout speed and price movement in the sales that followed a break.

2024
The November 2024 general sale for Glastonbury 2025 sold out in 35 minutes (BBC News, Nov 2024), the eighth consecutive sale to clear in under an hour.
2023
The November 2023 sale for Glastonbury 2024 sold out in 57 minutes, with the standard ticket priced at £355 plus booking fee.
2022
In the first sale after two pandemic years off, tickets for Glastonbury 2023 rose £55 year-on-year to £335 plus fee; the main allocation went in about an hour and the spring resale cleared in 6 minutes.
2012
After the 2012 fallow year, the October sale for Glastonbury 2013 sold out in 1 hour 40 minutes, a record for the festival at the time despite a then-record price of £205.
8 of 8
All 8 of the last 8 Glastonbury ticket sales sold out in under an hour. The open question for 2027 is not whether it sells out but when the sale date lands and how far the price jumps after two years of pent-up demand.

Every sale for eight editions has sold out inside an hour; post-pause sales bring the sharpest price rises.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case says the sale date lands after 10 August. Glastonbury's operational rhythm is remarkably stable: registration opens in summer, the sale date is confirmed in early autumn, and the sale itself runs in November. In the 2024 cycle the festival confirmed its November sale dates in October. Opening registration in July is exactly on that clock, and nothing in the fallow year changes the incentive to hold the announcement until registration numbers have built. The forecast leans NO on a date announcement inside this window.

The counter-case is that a fallow year is not a normal cycle. Organisers know 2027 demand will be the heaviest ever after a two-year wait, and an earlier, clearly signposted sale date is the cheapest way to manage a registration surge and the inevitable server strain. Emily Eavis has also used quiet years to make early announcements before. If the festival wants November's sale to run clean, telling 210,000-plus hopefuls the date before mid-August is a rational move, and a single Instagram post resolves this market YES.
