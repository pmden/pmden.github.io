Spain silence France in Dallas. The double is one match away.

Mikel Oyarzabal's penalty and a Pedro Porro strike sent Spain past France 2-0 and into the World Cup final at MetLife Stadium on 19 July.

4 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

Mikel Oyarzabal's first-half penalty and Pedro Porro's second-half strike gave Spain a 2-0 semi-final win over France in Dallas, sending the European champions into the World Cup final.

Sky Sports, 14 Jul 2026
2 · The event

How Spain closed the door on France

Spain reached the World Cup final by beating France 2-0 in Dallas on Tuesday. The opening goal came in the 22nd minute, after Lucas Digne caught Lamine Yamal while clearing inside the France box: Oyarzabal drove the penalty into the upper right corner for his fifth goal of the tournament, and Pedro Porro doubled the lead on 58 minutes, collecting Dani Olmo's pass and finishing past Mike Maignan (ESPN, 14 Jul 2026).

The result rested on Spain's control of France's front line. Kylian Mbappé, the tournament's joint-top scorer, found little room as Spain repeatedly crowded him in transition and constricted the passing lanes to top assist-maker Michael Olise (Sky Sports, 15 Jul 2026). Spain have now conceded a single goal in seven matches at this World Cup, to Belgium in the quarter-final.

The win extends Spain's unbeaten run to 37 matches across all competitions, a joint record for a European nation (Sky Sports, 14 Jul 2026). As reigning European champions, Spain now stand one match from a Euro-World double. Their opponent at MetLife Stadium on 19 July is decided tonight in Atlanta, where England meet Argentina.

3 · The pattern

What happens when European champions reach the next World Cup

Spain arrive at this final as holders of the European Championship. The record of reigning European champions at the following World Cup is longer on cautionary tales than coronations.

1974
West Germany, European champions of 1972, won the World Cup on home soil, beating the Netherlands 2-1 in the final — the first Euro-World double.
1982
West Germany, holders of Euro 1980, reached the final in Spain but lost 3-1 to Italy.
2002
France, champions of Euro 2000 and world champions of 1998, exited at the group stage without scoring a goal.
2010
Spain, Euro 2008 winners, beat the Netherlands 1-0 in Johannesburg — the second and last time a reigning European champion won the World Cup.
2014
Spain, holders of Euro 2012, went out at the group stage after losing 5-1 to the Netherlands.
2022
Italy, Euro 2020 champions, failed to qualify for the World Cup altogether.
2 of 14
2 of 14 reigning European champions have won the following World Cup — West Germany in 1974 and Spain in 2010.

Of the fourteen reigning European champions to precede Spain, two won the following World Cup and four reached its final.

5 · The read

The Hunch read

The case for Spain is defensive and structural. One goal conceded in seven matches, a 37-game unbeaten run, and a semi-final in which the tournament's most productive attack managed no goals all point one way. Spain have beaten the strongest side left in the draw before either potential finalist has been tested against them.

The counter is the base rate. Reigning European champions have converted the next World Cup twice in fourteen attempts, and finals are one-off events in which seven-match form guarantees nothing — West Germany in 1982 arrived as European champions and lost. Pricing Spain in the mid-40s reflects a strong favourite, not a certainty.
