England survive the Azteca. The quarter-final wall waits.

Ten-man England beat co-hosts Mexico 3-2 in a storm-delayed epic at the Estadio Azteca.

4 min read

Five goals, one red card, one hour of thunder

England beat Mexico 3-2 at the Estadio Azteca despite playing from the 54th minute with ten men, setting up a quarter-final against Norway in Miami.

(Sky Sports, 5 Jul 2026)

Delayed an hour by thunderstorms over Mexico City, the last-16 tie produced a modern classic. Jude Bellingham scored twice in the space of 98 seconds in the first half, either side of sustained Mexican pressure, before Julián Quiñones pulled the co-hosts level on the night's momentum swings. Jarell Quansah was then shown a straight red card in the 54th minute after a VAR review by referee Alireza Faghani (Al Jazeera, 6 Jul 2026).

Down to ten, England extended the lead rather than protecting it: Harry Kane converted a penalty for 3-1, his sixth goal of the tournament, before Raúl Jiménez answered from the spot to set up a frantic finish that England withstood (Sky Sports, 5 Jul 2026). Thomas Tuchel called it a heroic performance while publicly questioning the officiating, asking whether Mexico's penalty met the clear-and-obvious threshold for VAR intervention (Sky Sports, 5 Jul 2026).

The win eliminates the second of the three co-hosts and sends England to Miami for a quarter-final against Norway on Saturday 11 July, without the suspended Quansah. England have not faced a World Cup quarter-final since losing to France in 2022 — and the last eight has been the recurring site of English exits for three decades.

The Pattern

Since 1990, England have contested five World Cup quarter-finals. The split is honest and unflattering: two wins, three defeats, with penalties and fine margins deciding most of them.

2022
Lost 2-1 to France in Qatar; Kane converted one penalty and skied a second late on.
2018
Beat Sweden 2-0 in Samara — England's most comfortable quarter-final of the modern era.
2006
Drew 0-0 with Portugal after Wayne Rooney's red card, then lost the shootout 3-1.
2002
Led Brazil at half-time and lost 2-1 to Ronaldinho's long-range free-kick.
1990
Beat Cameroon 3-2 after extra time, with two Gary Lineker penalties rescuing the tie.
2 of 5
England have won two of five World Cup quarter-finals since 1990. The pattern is split: this is the round where English tournaments most often end, but not a wall they never climb.

England have won 2 of their last 5 World Cup quarter-finals — a genuine split.

The Read

The case for England compounds. They have now won a hostile away knockout tie at altitude with ten men, Bellingham is the tournament's form player, and Kane is one goal off the scoring lead. Norway, for all the Haaland threat, are the least decorated side left in England's half. A 29 per cent call on reaching the final prices England below France and only narrowly above Argentina; on squad depth and route, the forecast arguably runs light.

The honest counter is the base rate and the bodies. England have lost three of five quarter-finals since 1990, and the two they won came against Sweden and Cameroon, not against a side carrying the tournament's joint-top scorer. Quansah's suspension removes a first-choice defender for precisely the fixture where England need one most. Teams that empty the tank in a five-goal epic at 2,200 metres have historically paid for it six days later.
