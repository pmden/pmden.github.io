Apple04 Sep 2026

Apple's keynote is on 9 September. Six of the last seven September iPhones were in stores within eleven days.

Apple has invited the press to Apple Park on 9 September under the line 'Surprise and shine', the first launch under chief executive John Ternus, with a foldable iPhone widely expected. Since 2019 every iPhone announced in September went on sale within eleven days of the keynote; the one exception, in 2020, was not announced in September at all.

1 · Today

Apple sent invitations for a 9 September event at its Cupertino headquarters, the first product introduction under new chief executive John Ternus, where it is expected to unveil its first foldable iPhone alongside the iPhone 18 Pro.

Bloomberg, 26 Aug 2026
2 · The event

What happened

Apple's invitations went out on 26 August for an event on Wednesday 9 September at Apple Park, livestreamed from 10am Pacific. Bloomberg reported that the company would use it to introduce its first foldable iPhone and the iPhone 18 Pro line, with new Apple Watches and an Apple TV refresh also possible, and that the event marks the start of John Ternus's tenure as chief executive (Bloomberg, 26 Aug 2026).

The cadence that follows a keynote is the more predictable part. Last year Apple announced the iPhone 17 on 9 September, opened pre-orders on Friday 12 September and put the phone in stores on Friday 19 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2025). The same ten-day gap held in 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 and 2019.

A new form factor is where the cadence has broken before. In 2022 the larger iPhone 14 Plus shipped three weeks after its siblings (Apple, 7 Sep 2022), and in 2020 the whole line slipped to an October event (Apple, 13 Oct 2020). A foldable is a bigger departure than either.

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Seven September launch cycles, each documented in Apple's own press releases. The dataset is the gap between the keynote and the day the flagship iPhone reached stores.

2025
September 2025: keynote on 9 September, pre-orders on 12 September, in stores on 19 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2025).
2024
September 2024: keynote on 9 September, pre-orders on 13 September, in stores on 20 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2024).
2023
September 2023: keynote on 12 September, pre-orders on 15 September, in stores on 22 September (Apple, 12 Sep 2023).
2022
September 2022: keynote on 7 September, in stores on 16 September; the new iPhone 14 Plus form factor did not ship until 7 October (Apple, 7 Sep 2022).
2021
September 2021: keynote on 14 September, pre-orders on 17 September, in stores on 24 September (Apple, 14 Sep 2021).
2020
October 2020: no September iPhone. The iPhone 12 was announced on 13 October and reached stores on 23 October (Apple, 13 Oct 2020).
2019
September 2019: keynote on 10 September, pre-orders on 13 September, in stores on 20 September (Apple, 10 Sep 2019).
6 of 7
In 6 of the last 7 launch cycles the flagship iPhone was in stores within eleven days of its keynote; the exception is 2020, when the pandemic pushed the whole launch to October. The record for new form factors is weaker: the iPhone 14 Plus in 2022 shipped a month after its keynote. A foldable is the largest change to the iPhone's shape since 2007.

Six of seven flagships in stores within eleven days; new form factors have slipped.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is that the foldable appears on stage and the Pro models follow the ten-day clock. Apple's cadence has held in six of seven years and the invitations are out; the announcement market is priced accordingly. The availability market is the one the history argues against: the only new form factor in the set shipped a month late, and foldable displays have constrained every manufacturer that has tried them.

The honest counter-case is that a new chief executive may prefer a clean launch to a fast one, announcing the foldable with a later ship date the way the Plus was handled in 2022. The announcement prediction fails only if the reporting is wrong; the pre-order prediction fails if Apple chooses October, which is the pattern for everything in this set that was not a straightforward annual refresh.
