Methodology

How we build every Echo

Every Echo follows the same six-step process. Pattern recognition over opinion. Multiple sources over single citation. Verified before published, not after.

The TEE pattern

Every body paragraph in every Echo follows the same structure: Topic, Evidence, Explanatory. A topic sentence states the claim. Evidence supports it with citation. An explanatory line ties it back to the central pattern. This is the same discipline used in academic essay writing — and it gates editorial quality before publication.

The six-step Echo pipeline

1
Source ingestion
30+ UK and international publications via APIs, scrapers, and archived databases. Hansard (Parliament record from 1803), UK National Archives, ONS, Bank of England, plus broadsheet press archives back to 1900.
2
Pattern detection
AI classifies current events into recurring patterns identified from the historical archive. Bills with similar dynamics. Polling collapses that preceded reshuffles. Policy announcements that fit "wind-down" trajectories. Pattern library at /patterns.
3
Echo draft generation
First draft written in TEE format across three blocks: today's news, historical pattern with cases, market hypothesis with base rates. Each paragraph cited inline (Harvard style).
4
Dual verification
Every factual claim cross-checked across minimum 2 independent sources. Different publications, not multiple copies of the same wire report. Statistics confirmed from authoritative source (ONS, Bank of England, IFS, etc.). Discrepancies flagged for editorial review.
5
Anti-fake check
Automated classifier flags retracted claims, known hoaxes, and AI-generated noise. Cross-reference against Snopes, FactCheck, and publication retraction databases. Anything flagged routed to editor.
6
Editorial pass
Human editor reviews draft for tone, factual nuance, and Editorial Code compliance. No Echo publishes without explicit editor sign-off. Editor's name appears on every Echo.

Numbers

  • 125 years of UK political news archive coverage
  • 30+ publications referenced across each Echo
  • 3 minimum independent sources per factual claim
  • ~3-5 Echoes published per week post-launch
  • 24-hour latency from event to Echo publication (target)

What we will NOT do

  • Publish opinion without evidence backing
  • Take partisan editorial positions on policy questions
  • Use direct quotes from primary sources without paraphrasing the analysis in our own words
  • Recommend market positions (we publish predictions, you make decisions)
  • Trade against our own published markets
