Fiscal devolution03 Sep 2026

England is promised a tourist tax for 2028. Scotland legislated one in 2024.

The Prime Minister told the Commons on 1 September that fiscal devolution will start with the power to set an overnight visitor levy, to commence towards the end of the 2027-28 financial year. Scotland's Act received Royal Assent in July 2024 and Wales's in September 2025.

4 min readEcho #116UK
1 · Today

In a Commons statement the Prime Minister says devolution will include fiscal devolution, starting with the ability to set an overnight visitor levy, to commence towards the end of the financial year 2027-28.

GOV.UK, 1 Sep 2026
2 · The event

A power named, a date attached, and a queue already formed

The Prime Minister's statement extended devolution offers to every part of England without devolved power and said the package would include powers long requested by mayors, naming post-16 technical education, employment support and fiscal devolution, and specifying that fiscal devolution starts with the ability to set an overnight visitor levy to commence towards the end of the financial year 2027-28 (GOV.UK, 1 Sep 2026). The statement attaches those powers to mayors.

The other two legislatures got there first, and their Acts are on the statute book rather than in a speech. The Visitor Levy (Scotland) Act 2024 was passed by the Scottish Parliament on 28 May 2024 and received Royal Assent on 5 July 2024, enabling local authorities to impose a levy on people staying overnight in certain types of accommodation (legislation.gov.uk, 2024). The Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025 received Royal Assent on 18 September 2025, creating a register of accommodation providers and giving principal councils the power to levy overnight stays (legislation.gov.uk, 2025).

Wales has since legislated again on the same subject. The Development of Tourism and Regulation of Visitor Accommodation (Wales) Act 2026 was one of eleven Acts passed in the final months of the Sixth Senedd. So by the time an English mayor is able to charge a visitor a pound a night, the devolved nations will have had the power for between two and four years and Wales will have amended its version twice.

3 · The pattern

Devolved taxes legislated in Scotland and Wales, and what England got

Inclusion rule, fixed before the count: this pattern counts every new tax or levy for which the Scottish Parliament or the Senedd has passed a substantive Act since 2013, as listed on the statute book. The test is stated in advance: has an equivalent power to charge that tax been conferred on any English local or combined authority. Acts that administer or amend an existing devolved tax are excluded, because the power already existed. UK-wide taxes are excluded, because they were never devolved to anyone.

2017
Land transaction tax. The Land Transaction Tax and Anti-avoidance of Devolved Taxes (Wales) Act 2017 gave Wales its own property transaction tax, following Scotland's in 2013. No English authority has an equivalent power; stamp duty remains a UK tax collected by HMRC.
2017
Landfill disposals tax. The Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017 replaced UK landfill tax in Wales, again following Scotland. No English authority sets or keeps it.
2024
Aggregates tax. The Aggregates Tax and Devolved Taxes Administration (Scotland) Act 2024 created a Scottish tax on commercially exploited aggregate. No English authority has the equivalent.
2024
Visitor levy. Scotland's Act received Royal Assent on 5 July 2024 and Wales's on 18 September 2025. England's version was promised on 1 September 2026, for the end of the 2027-28 financial year, and has no Bill behind it yet.
0 of 4
0 of 4. Two honest qualifications. Four cases is the floor for a base rate, and the comparison is asymmetric by construction: Scotland and Wales have legislatures that can create taxes and English authorities do not, so the record partly restates the constitution rather than predicting behaviour. What it does measure is the willingness of successive UK governments to hand an English authority a tax base, and on that the answer for thirteen years has been no.

0 of the 4 devolved taxes legislated in Scotland or Wales since 2013 has produced an equivalent power for an English authority. The visitor levy is the first to be promised, three to four years after the devolved Acts.

What this pattern excludes

The business rates retention arrangements in England
They change how the proceeds of an existing national tax are shared, not who may create or set a tax. Counting them would let a funding formula score as a tax power.
Council tax and the workplace parking levy
English authorities do set council tax, and Nottingham has run a workplace parking levy since 2012, but both predate the 2013 start of this window and neither was created by a devolved Act. The window is set by the first Scottish devolved tax legislation, not by the answer.
Acts amending or administering an existing devolved tax
The Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016 and similar measures build machinery for taxes already devolved. Including them would count the same devolution twice.

5 · The read

The read

The fortnight market is low because the statement did the work a statement does. A Prime Minister naming a tax power in the Commons is a signal to mayors and to the Treasury, not the start of a drafting exercise, and the date attached to it sits eighteen months out. Departments do not publish rate structures for a levy that commences at the end of the following financial year.

The two later markets test something more interesting, which is whether this promise behaves like the previous four. In each of those cases a devolved legislature legislated and Westminster did not follow for England, not because it refused in public but because nothing was ever introduced. The tell here is the third market rather than the second. Naming the authorities is the cheap half of the job and the half a government does when it means it, because it converts a general offer into a list of places that can start planning. If the list has not appeared by the spring, the 2027-28 date is doing the same work the previous four promises did, which is to defer.
