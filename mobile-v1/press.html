Press notes

Hunch builds on the same prediction-market mechanics used by Polymarket and Kalshi, but with an editorial-first product design tailored to UK broadsheet readers. We are not a sportsbook; we are not a casino; we are a publishing platform with prediction as the action layer.

Where the prediction-market category in the US has grown around event-driven retail traders, Hunch targets the FT/Times reader — someone who already reads political analysis and wants to test their judgement against the patterns. Reputation is the product; money is a later layer.
