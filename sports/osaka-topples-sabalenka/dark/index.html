Wimbledon06 Jul 2026

Sabalenka falls. Wimbledon belongs to no one.

Naomi Osaka beat world No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka 6-2, 7-6(2) in the fourth round, wiping the last of the top three seeds from the women's draw.

4 min read

What happened

Naomi Osaka beat world No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka 6-2, 7-6(2) in the fourth round — Sabalenka's first straight-sets defeat at a major since the 2020 US Open.

(ESPN, 5 Jul 2026)

Fourteenth seed Naomi Osaka produced the result of the fortnight on Centre Court, dismissing world No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka 6-2, 7-6(2) to reach the first Wimbledon quarter-final of her career. Sabalenka had won the pair's last three meetings, including a straight-sets fourth-round win at Roland Garros only weeks earlier, and had not lost in straight sets at a Grand Slam since the 2020 US Open (ESPN, 5 July 2026).

The defeat completed a clear-out at the top of the women's draw. Second seed Elena Rybakina and third seed Iga Swiatek had already gone out, so Sabalenka's exit leaves none of the top three seeds in the last eight, per the official Wimbledon draw. Every woman still playing is chasing a first Wimbledon title, which guarantees a ninth consecutive first-time champion at the All England Club.

Osaka, a four-time major champion who had never previously passed the third round on grass, next faces tenth seed Karolina Muchova for a place in the semi-finals. The women's semi-finals are scheduled for 9 July and the final for 11 July.

The Pattern

What happens at Wimbledon once the leading seeds are gone: the title has not gone back to a former champion since 2016, and it has repeatedly gone to a player the seedings did not rank near the front. The test below is the one the first market asks — was the champion seeded outside the top ten? Reports from ESPN, the WTA and the official Wimbledon records.

2025
Iga Swiatek won her first Wimbledon, dismantling Amanda Anisimova 6-0, 6-0 in the most one-sided final of the Open Era.
2024
Barbora Krejcikova, seeded 31st, beat Jasmine Paolini in three sets for her first Wimbledon title.
2023
Marketa Vondrousova became the first unseeded woman in the Open Era to win Wimbledon, beating Ons Jabeur in the final.
2022
Elena Rybakina, seeded 17th, came from a set down to beat Jabeur for her first major title.
3 of 4
Three of the last four Wimbledon women's champions were seeded outside the top ten: Krejcikova at 31, Vondrousova unseeded, Rybakina at 17. The exception is the most recent, Swiatek in 2025, who arrived near the top of the rankings. The other half of the record is settled before a ball is struck this week: every champion since Ashleigh Barty's first title in 2021 has been a first-time winner, eight in a row, and with no former champion left in the 2026 draw that streak reaches nine whatever happens.

Three of the last four Wimbledon women's champions were seeded outside the top ten; all eight since 2017 were first-time winners.

The Read

The weight of the pattern favours the outsider. Three of the last four champions were seeded 17th, 31st and not at all, eight straight winners have been first-timers, and the 2026 draw has lost its top three seeds before the quarter-finals. That is why the first market opens above even money: this tournament has been rewarding whoever holds their nerve in week two rather than whoever the rankings prefer. Osaka, seeded 14th, sits on the outsider side of that line herself, and the title market's 24% on her reflects a four-time major champion with a serve built for fast grass.

The counter-case is that the seedings have not stopped mattering: the most recent final went to a player from the top of the list, and tenth seed Muchova, an all-court player with a Roland Garros final behind her, is exactly the awkward type who has troubled big hitters at this event. Osaka's own grass record remains thin, this being her first Wimbledon quarter-final in eight visits, and one statement win over Sabalenka does not overwrite years of early exits on the surface. The seeding market fails if the draw's remaining top-ten players simply take over now that the very top is gone.
