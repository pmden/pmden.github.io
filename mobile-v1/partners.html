Partners

Brand partnerships and sponsored Echoes

Hunch reaches UK broadsheet readers who already pay for political analysis. Three ways to work with us: Paid Echo, Pattern Briefing, Member events.

Hunch publishes one Paid Echo per fortnight maximum. Every sponsored piece is editorially designed: same TEE structure, same source-verification, same byline. Clearly labelled. No reuse of our independent editorial under a sponsor banner. The model is FT Partner Studio — brand association with rigour, not advertorial.

Tier 1

Paid Echo · from £15k

A single sponsored analytical Echo built around a topic the sponsor cares about. Same editorial discipline as an independent Echo. Three-week production. One-month homepage rotation. Permanent archive.

  • Dedicated Echo with sponsor branding above the byline
  • Topic alignment workshop with our editor before commissioning
  • Distribution: homepage, newsletter (75k by Q4 2026), social
  • Performance report: views, time-on-page, prediction volume, share counts
Tier 2

Pattern Briefing · from £8k / quarter

A quarterly closed briefing for the sponsor's leadership team or clients. Three patterns of current relevance, with case studies and prediction implications. Delivered in person, virtually, or as a private memo.

  • Bespoke pattern selection based on sponsor industry
  • 60-minute interactive briefing or 4,000-word memo
  • Q&A with founder & editor
  • White-label PDF for internal distribution
Tier 3

Hunch Salon · from £25k / event

A private dinner or convening with a curated guest list: senior journalists, policy professionals, City decision-makers. Sponsor co-hosts. Off the record. Hunch handles editorial selection and invitations.

  • 30-60 invited guests in central London
  • Venue, F&B, programme curated by Hunch
  • Co-branded run-of-show & collateral
  • Optional post-event briefing memo
