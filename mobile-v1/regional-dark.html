The Prime Minister told the Commons on 1 September that work has begun with Cornwall Council and its six MPs on a devolution deal, and that the deal need not come with an elected mayor. The same statement reserved fiscal powers, including a visitor levy, for mayors.

Northern Ireland heats itself with oil, and it has built three household energy support schemes since 2023. Every one of them opened on the day the Department finally named. Every one of them took months to reach that day.

Northern Ireland's jobless rate is under 2 per cent. Four in ten adults are outside the labour market.

Only 17 of the 376 rolling quarters NISRA has published since 1995 have shown unemployment below 2 per cent, and every one of them has come since 2023. The low rate and the high inactivity rate are the same fact seen from two sides, and both are measured in the release due in twelve days.

Drought01 Aug

The fields ran dry in July. History says the till pays in winter.

Wholesale iceberg lettuce is up 90%, tomatoes 60% and potatoes 40% as drought halves broccoli yields and sends buyers to Spain and the Netherlands in peak British season. The intuitive bet is that food inflation jumps in the very next ONS print. In three of the four drought summers since 1976, that is exactly what did not happen. In 1995, the driest on record, it did.
