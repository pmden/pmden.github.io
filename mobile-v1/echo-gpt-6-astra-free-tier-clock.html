OpenAI04 Sep 2026

GPT-6 starts with the cyber programme. Two of OpenAI's last four flagships reached free users on day one.

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on 3 September to companies in its cybersecurity programme first, with paid ChatGPT tiers and the API promised over the coming days. Of the last four flagship models, two were free to all ChatGPT users at launch and two started behind a paywall.

4 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra, describing it as its most intelligent and aligned model, with a staged rollout starting with organisations in its cybersecurity programme, then paid ChatGPT plans and the API over the coming days.

OpenAI, 3 Sep 2026
2 · The event

What happened

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra on 3 September as a generational step in computer use, software engineering, professional work and science, and the first model it has slowed for cyber-risk review before release (OpenAI, 3 Sep 2026). Access is staged: companies in the application-based cybersecurity programme first, then ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise over the coming days, then the API and Amazon Web Services (Bloomberg, 3 Sep 2026).

A second version with added cybersecurity guardrails is planned for paid ChatGPT users, and both versions carry safeguards on the model's most advanced offensive capabilities (Bloomberg, 3 Sep 2026). Free ChatGPT users do not appear in the announced schedule.

That omission is the question. OpenAI's flagship launches have split between two models of distribution: paid-first, with the free tier waiting weeks or months, and free-at-launch, when the company wanted scale at once. The last four flagships divide two and two.

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Four flagship model launches since 2023, each documented in OpenAI's own announcement. The dataset is who could use the model on day one.

2025
August 2025: GPT-5 became the default model for all ChatGPT users, including the free tier, on 7 August (OpenAI, 7 Aug 2025).
2025
February 2025: the GPT-4.5 research preview went to ChatGPT Pro subscribers on 27 February, with Plus and Team the following week and no free-tier access at launch (OpenAI, 27 Feb 2025).
2024
May 2024: GPT-4o was announced on 13 May as free for all ChatGPT users, with browsing, data analysis and memory opened to the free tier at the same time (OpenAI, 13 May 2024).
2023
March 2023: GPT-4 launched on 14 March for ChatGPT Plus subscribers with a usage cap, and a waitlist for the API (OpenAI, 14 Mar 2023).
2 of 4
In 2 of the last 4 flagship launches the model was free to all ChatGPT users on day one: GPT-4o in 2024 and GPT-5 in 2025. GPT-4 in 2023 and GPT-4.5 in 2025 started with paying subscribers. The split follows a rule: the models OpenAI positioned as its most capable frontier release went paid-first; the ones positioned for scale went free. GPT-6 Astra is being described in the first language.

Two of four flagships free on day one; the two paid-first launches were the frontier models.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is paid-first. The announcement lists every paid tier and the API and does not list the free plan; the model is being sold as a frontier capability with a cyber-risk story attached, which is the profile of GPT-4 and GPT-4.5, not GPT-4o and GPT-5. That is why the free-tier market sits below a third and the API market above 60%.

The honest counter-case is that OpenAI has used free access to blunt competitor launches before, and did so with GPT-5 only thirteen months ago. A limited-rate free variant would satisfy the market's wording. The prediction fails if the company decides scale matters more than the safeguards story within the fortnight, or if the guardrailed version described for paid users turns out to be the free one.
