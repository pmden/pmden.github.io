What is Brier score?

Brier score measures the accuracy of probabilistic predictions. A perfect forecaster scores 0; a forecaster who always guesses 50/50 on binary questions scores 0.25; a forecaster who is reliably wrong scores 1. Lower is better. We use Brier rather than profit because it rewards calibration over luck — being right with appropriate confidence, repeatedly. See full methodology.

How to climb the leaderboard

  • Predict consistently — only resolved markets count, and you need 25 to qualify for ranking
  • Calibration matters more than direction — say 70% if you mean 70%, not 95%
  • Specialise — separate leaderboards for Politics, Fiscal, Policy reward depth
  • Streaks are bonus signal, not scoring — 5+ consecutive correct adds visibility
