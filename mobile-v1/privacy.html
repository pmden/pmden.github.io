Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we don't. UK GDPR-compliant, plain English first.

Last updated: 5 June 2026 · Version 1.0 · Data controller: Krok Group Ltd

TL;DR

We collect the minimum data needed to run Hunch. We never sell your data. We never share it with third-party advertisers. Email is private. Display name and predictions are public. You can export or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

Email address
For login, account notifications, newsletter. Private. Never sold.
Display name
Public. Appears on leaderboard and beside your predictions.
Predictions & Brier score
Public. The point of the product is your public record.
Login & security data
Hashed password, sessions, IP address (logged for 30 days for security only).
Usage analytics
Pages viewed, anonymised. Self-hosted analytics. No third-party trackers.
Payment data (Members)
Processed by Stripe. We store only billing reference and plan. We never see card numbers.

What we don't collect

  • Phone number (unless you choose to share for 2FA)
  • Physical address (unless required for paid services)
  • Third-party advertising identifiers
  • Cross-site browsing behaviour
  • Social-graph data beyond your own connections within the platform

How we use your data

  • Provide the service (account, predictions, leaderboard)
  • Send service emails (account, security, important changes)
  • Send newsletter (only if you opt in; one-click unsubscribe in every email)
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
  • Improve the product through aggregated, anonymised analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations (regulators, court orders where compelled)

Third parties we use

  • Stripe — payment processing (Members only)
  • Email provider — transactional email and newsletter delivery
  • Hosting provider — cloud infrastructure (data resident in EU/UK)

We have data processing agreements with each. Full vendor list available on request.

Your rights under UK GDPR

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — delete your account and associated data
  • Portability — export your data in machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing for specific purposes
  • Withdraw consent — for marketing/newsletter at any time

Exercise any of these from your dashboard or by writing to privacy@byhunch.com. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies (login session, security, theme preference). No third-party advertising cookies. No tracking pixels. Full cookie list at .

Data retention

  • Account data: kept while account is active, deleted within 30 days of closure
  • Predictions: retained as part of public record (anonymised after account deletion)
  • Logs and security data: 30 days
  • Financial records: 7 years (UK statutory requirement)

International transfers

Your data is stored in the UK and EU. Any transfers outside happen under UK GDPR-compliant safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses).

Children

Hunch is for 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Complaints

If you believe we've handled your data improperly, write to privacy@byhunch.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at .

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be notified by email and in your dashboard at least 30 days before they take effect. Version history available on request.
