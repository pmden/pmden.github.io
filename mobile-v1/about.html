About

A new kind of analytical media

Hunch is a UK political prediction publishing platform. We pair editorial analysis with prediction markets — same mechanics as Polymarket, completely different product. Built for broadsheet readers who want to read first and predict second.

What we do

Hunch publishes Echoes — analytical pieces that combine three things: a current UK political event, the historical pattern it fits, and a market question derived from that pattern. Each Echo is built on 125 years of news archives, cross-referenced across 30+ publications, and double-verified before publication.

Readers can predict the outcome of any Echo using Hunch Points — a skill-based prediction layer that rewards accuracy over time. From 2027, real-money markets become available pending UK Gambling Commission approval.

Why we exist

UK political content has two extremes. On one side, broadsheet analysis — deep but with no way to act on insight. On the other, betting platforms — actionable but with no editorial depth. Hunch fits the gap: rigorous editorial that ends with a prediction, owned by you, scored over time.

Our premise: readers want more than to consume political news. They want to test their own thinking against the patterns. They want their accuracy public. They want to be early to consequential events, not commentary after the fact.

Who reads Hunch

Broadsheet readers, 45+. People who already subscribe to FT, Times, Telegraph, Guardian, Economist. People who follow Westminster closely. People who form views and want a place to test them.

We are not building for crypto-native traders. We are not building for casual punters. We are building for serious people who treat politics as something worth reading carefully — and who want to know whether their reading was correct.

How we are different

  • Publishing-first. Echoes are the atomic unit. Markets are derived from Echoes, not the other way around.
  • Pattern library. 125 years of UK political archives, organised into recurring dynamics with case banks. This is our IP.
  • Public reputation. Predictions are public. Accuracy is tracked via Brier score. Leaderboards make standing visible.
  • Editorial discipline. Every claim cited. No direct opinion. Double-verified. Editorial Code of Practice applies to every piece.
  • UK-first, then Europe. Vertical focus year one: UK fiscal and political prediction. Year three: European expansion via country-specific Hunches.

The team

Hunch is built by Pavlo Denysiuk (founder, Ukrainian, 8 years regulated Web3, ex-CEO Lunu Pay) and Alexander (CTO). Backed by editorial advisors and operational support from a multi-agent AI stack. More on the team →

The institution we are building

Our 20-year ambition: become the FT-tier analytical media institution for civic and political decision-making. What Bloomberg did for financial decision-makers in the 1980s and 90s, we intend to do for thoughtful political readers in the 2020s and beyond.

The path: UK political prediction first. European expansion second. Multi-vertical (finance, sports, culture) third. Print, podcast, and physical presence (Hunch House) over time.

This is a long project. We invite readers who want to be part of it from the start.
