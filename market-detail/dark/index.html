Echo · Historical Pattern Read full Echo →
Contested bills under similar pressure tend to pass — but the recess deadline matters most.
In 3 of the last 4 cases where the Lords returned a major bill with 10+ amendments mid-session, the Commons passed it within four weeks. The exception — Thatcher's Shops Bill (1986) — was defeated when 72 Tory MPs rebelled against a free-Sunday-trading provision. The Online Safety Bill's amendments are technical, not values-based.
