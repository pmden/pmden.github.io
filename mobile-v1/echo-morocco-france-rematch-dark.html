World Cup04 Jul 2026

Ounahi lights up Houston. Doha's unfinished business is France.

Azzedine Ounahi's double swept Morocco past co-hosts Canada 3-0 and into a quarter-final against France — a rematch of the 2022 semi-final the Atlas Lions lost in Doha.

4 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

Ounahi scored twice as Morocco beat Canada 3-0 in Houston, making the co-hosts the first of the three home nations to exit and booking a quarter-final with France.

Al Jazeera, 4 Jul 2026
2 · The event

A second-half demolition, a familiar opponent

After a pedestrian first half in Houston, Morocco accelerated. Azzedine Ounahi collected Achraf Hakimi's free-kick and drove a shot through traffic into the bottom corner, added a second from Brahim Díaz's counter-attack pass in the 82nd minute, and Díaz then set up substitute Soufiane Rahimi in stoppage time — a fourth World Cup assist that set a new African record (Al Jazeera, 4 Jul 2026). Canada became the first of the three co-hosts eliminated (Sky Sports, 4 Jul 2026).

Morocco's path here already carried a statement: they knocked out the Netherlands on penalties in the round of 32, winning the shootout 3-2 after a 1-1 draw. The 2022 semi-finalists are the only African side ever to reach a World Cup semi-final, and they are now one match from repeating it at consecutive tournaments.

The opponent sharpens the story. France beat Paraguay 1-0 in Philadelphia through Kylian Mbappé's 69th-minute penalty — his seventh goal of the tournament — to set up the Boston quarter-final on Thursday 9 July (Al Jazeera, 4 Jul 2026). France won the sides' 2022 semi-final 2-0 in Doha. Morocco have waited three and a half years for the rematch.

3 · The pattern

World Cup rematches repeat themselves

When a World Cup knockout tie is rematched at the following edition, the side that won the first meeting has won again in every recent case. The sample is small, but it is unbroken.

2022
Argentina beat the Netherlands on penalties (4-3 after 2-2) in the quarter-final — repeating their 2014 semi-final shootout win over the same opponent.
2014
Germany beat Argentina 1-0 in the final, having beaten them 4-0 in the 2010 quarter-final.
2014
Brazil beat Chile on penalties (3-2 after 1-1) in the last 16, repeating their 3-0 win over Chile at the same stage in 2010.
2010
Germany thrashed Argentina 4-0 in the quarter-final, having already knocked them out on penalties in the 2006 quarter-final.
4 of 4
In the last four World Cup knockout rematches, the side that won the first meeting won again every time. The pattern is unanimous — and it favours France, who beat Morocco 2-0 in the 2022 semi-final.

In 4 of 4 recent World Cup knockout rematches, the previous winner won again.

5 · The read

The Hunch read

The rematch pattern is as one-sided as base rates get: four of four previous winners won again, and France arrive with Mbappé joint-top of the scoring charts and a knockout win banked without conceding. Morocco's 2022 run also ended precisely here against precisely this opponent. The forecast that history repeats on Thursday is the disciplined one.

The counter-case rests on trajectory. The 2022 Morocco defended their way to a semi-final; this edition scores — three against Canada, a shootout win over the Netherlands, and a new African assist record for Brahim Díaz. France needed a VAR-awarded penalty to pass Paraguay and have looked less fluent than their results. A 34 per cent call on Morocco is well above what the rematch pattern alone would imply, and the gap between those two numbers is where this market's interest lives.
