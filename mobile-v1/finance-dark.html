Tokyo spent 15 trillion yen in a month. The Bank of Japan has answered an intervention with a hike once in four.

Japan's finance ministry disclosed 15.4 trillion yen of yen-buying between 30 July and 26 August, after the first joint operation with Washington since 2011, and the US Treasury has publicly backed 'decisive' monetary steps. Of four previous intervention episodes, only 2024 was followed by a rate rise at the next Bank of Japan meeting.

ECB04 Sep

The ECB has raised rates into an energy shock four times. Three were reversed within a year.

Eurostat's flash estimate put August inflation at 3.3%, up from 2.9%, with energy at 14.3%. The Governing Council meets on 10 September. In 2000, 2008 and 2011 the ECB raised rates with energy costs driving inflation and reversed course within three to seven months; in 2022 the hiking cycle lasted two years before the first cut.

Its next monthly meeting falls three days after Iran struck US bases in four countries. In four earlier attacks on Gulf energy and military targets, OPEC+ did not tighten at its next meeting in response.
