The Rothko clears $85.8m. The art market exhales.

Mark Rothko's Brown and Blacks in Reds (1957), from the estate of dealer Robert Mnuchin, made $85.8m at Sotheby's in New York, leading a $433.1m contemporary evening sale — more than double the equivalent sale a year earlier.

4 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

The Mnuchin-estate Rothko sold for $85.8m, leading Sotheby's $433.1m contemporary evening sale — resolution recorded against the house's published results.

Sotheby's results (official), 14 May 2026
2 · The event

What happened

Sotheby's contemporary evening sale in New York totalled $433.1m, led by Mark Rothko's Brown and Blacks in Reds (1957) from the estate of the dealer and banker Robert Mnuchin. The canvas hammered at $74m against a $70m–$100m estimate and reached $85.8m with fees — the second-highest price ever paid for a Rothko at auction (Sotheby's published results, May 2026).

The result landed in a season the trade had billed as a turning point. Works estimated at a combined $1.8bn–$2.6bn came to market across Christie's, Sotheby's, Phillips and Bonhams in May, with Sotheby's low estimate alone running 70% ahead of its May 2025 hammer total (The New York Times, 13 May 2026). The two houses split the season's marquee estates: Christie's took the Marian Goodman collection, headlined by a Gerhard Richter estimated at $35m–$50m; Sotheby's took Mnuchin's.

The Rothko fell just short of the artist's record — the $86.9m paid for Orange, Red, Yellow in 2012 — but it more than doubled Sotheby's equivalent sale from a year earlier, which had tallied $186.1m (The New York Times, 13 May 2026).

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Marquee single-owner estates are how the auction market restarts itself after a slump: guaranteed, museum-grade material draws out bidders who ignored two years of mixed-quality sales. The estates almost always perform. The question each time is whether the season around them does.

2025
The Leonard Lauder collection at Sotheby's produced the sale of Gustav Klimt's portrait of Elisabeth Lederer for $236.4m — the second-most expensive work ever sold at auction — in an otherwise cautious November season.
2024
The counter-example: the same May week produced roughly $1.4bn across the New York houses, down more than a fifth year on year, as sellers withheld consignments and estimates were trimmed.
2022
The Paul Allen collection at Christie's made $1.62bn in a single evening series — the first collection ever to clear $1bn at auction — even as the broader market cooled.
2018
The Rockefeller estate at Christie's totalled $835m, then a record for a single collection, and pulled the whole spring season up with it.
2012
Rothko's Orange, Red, Yellow set the artist's record at $86.9m in a post-crisis market that trophy sales helped reignite — a mark the Mnuchin canvas nearly matched fourteen years later.
3 of 5
The pattern is split and should be read as split: marquee estates beat their low estimates in four of five recent cases, but the broader season total rose year on year in only three of five. A great Rothko night does not guarantee a great half-year.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case for recovery is supply. The art market's two down years were a consignment strike, not a demand collapse: owners of great material refused to sell into soft estimates. The Mnuchin and Goodman estates broke that logjam, the Rothko found bidding well past its low estimate, and Sotheby's evening total more than doubling year on year is exactly what the front edge of a rebound looks like. Christie's half-year figure, due in the last weeks of July, is the first season-wide print that can confirm it.

The honest counter is that estates flatter the average. Nearly every major lot in May carried a guarantee or irrevocable submission, meaning the headline prices measure dealmaking before the auction as much as bidding in the room. The middle market — the sub-$1m lots that carry the houses' volume — remained soft through spring, and summer is the market's thinnest season: an 18% chance of another $50m lot before mid-August is generous. One spectacular Rothko night can coexist with a half-year number that is merely flat.
