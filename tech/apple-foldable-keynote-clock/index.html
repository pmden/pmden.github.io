Apple27 Aug 2026

Apple's keynote is 9 September. The last eight iPhones shipped by that Friday.

Apple has invited the press to Apple Park on 9 September under the line 'Surprise and shine', the first launch under chief executive John Ternus.

4 min read

What happened

Apple set 9 September as the date of its next iPhone launch event, the first major product event under new chief executive John Ternus; Bloomberg reported the same day that a first foldable iPhone is expected alongside the iPhone 18 Pro.

(Reuters, 26 Aug 2026)

Apple's invitations went out on 26 August for an event on Wednesday 9 September at Apple Park, livestreamed from 10am Pacific, the first major product event under chief executive John Ternus (Reuters, 26 Aug 2026). Bloomberg reported that the company would use it to introduce its first foldable iPhone and the iPhone 18 Pro line, with new Apple Watches and an Apple TV refresh also possible (Bloomberg, 26 Aug 2026).

The cadence that follows a keynote is the more predictable part. Last year Apple announced the iPhone 17 on Tuesday 9 September, opened pre-orders on Friday 12 September and put the phone in stores on Friday 19 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2025). The same second-Friday rhythm held in 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020, 2019 and 2018 (Apple, 9 Sep 2024) (Apple, 12 Sep 2018). For a Wednesday 9 September keynote that Friday is 18 September.

A new form factor is where the cadence has broken before. In 2022 the larger iPhone 14 Plus shipped three weeks after its siblings (Apple, 7 Sep 2022), and in 2020 the whole line slipped to an October event (Apple, 13 Oct 2020). A foldable is a bigger departure than either.

The Pattern

Fourteen launch cycles since 2012, each documented in Apple's own press releases. The dataset is the gap between the keynote and the day the base flagship iPhone reached stores. Held = in stores by the second Friday after the keynote, at most eleven days.

2025
September 2025: keynote on 9 September, pre-orders on 12 September, in stores on 19 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2025).
2024
September 2024: keynote on 9 September, pre-orders on 13 September, in stores on 20 September (Apple, 9 Sep 2024).
2023
September 2023: keynote on 12 September, pre-orders on 15 September, in stores on 22 September (Apple, 12 Sep 2023).
2022
September 2022: keynote on 7 September, in stores on 16 September; the new iPhone 14 Plus did not ship until 7 October, 30 days after the keynote (Apple, 7 Sep 2022).
2021
September 2021: keynote on 14 September, pre-orders on 17 September, in stores on 24 September (Apple, 14 Sep 2021).
2020
October 2020: the pandemic pushed the event to 13 October, but the iPhone 12 still reached stores on 23 October, ten days later; the 12 mini followed on 13 November (Apple, 13 Oct 2020).
2019
September 2019: keynote on 10 September, pre-orders on 13 September, in stores on 20 September (Apple, 10 Sep 2019).
2018
September 2018: keynote on 12 September, iPhone XS in stores on 21 September; the new iPhone XR did not reach stores until 26 October, 44 days after the keynote (Apple, 12 Sep 2018).
8 of 8
Eight of the last eight base flagship iPhones were in Apple stores by the second Friday after the keynote, never more than eleven days. Across all fourteen September-cycle launches since 2012 it is thirteen of fourteen; the exception is 2015, when the iPhone 6s took sixteen days. The 2020 event slipped to October but the phone still shipped in ten days. New shapes have run slower: the iPhone X took 52 days in 2017, the XR 44 days in 2018 and the 14 Plus 30 days in 2022.

Eight of the last eight flagships in stores by the second Friday; thirteen of fourteen since 2012. New form factors have slipped.

The Read

The weighted case is that whatever Apple announces on 9 September follows the second-Friday clock into stores on 18 September. The cadence has held in eight of eight cycles since 2018 and in thirteen of fourteen since 2012, and the invitations are out; the first market opens at 80 because the one miss, 2015, was a plain annual refresh with no obvious cause.

The foldable questions run against the record. The only new shapes in the set, the X in 2017, the XR in 2018, the 12 mini in 2020 and the 14 Plus in 2022, all missed the second Friday by weeks. Bloomberg's reporting gives the foldable a place on stage, and a new chief executive may still choose to ship everything at once; but the pre-order and in-store predictions for the foldable hold only if Apple breaks its own habit of holding back the unfamiliar model.
