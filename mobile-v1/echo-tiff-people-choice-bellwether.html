TIFF opens on Thursday. Six of the last seven People's Choice winners reached the Best Picture list.

The 51st Toronto International Film Festival runs from 10 to 20 September, opening with Siân Heder's Being Heumann at Roy Thomson Hall. Its People's Choice Award, announced on the final Sunday, has produced six Best Picture nominees and two winners in the last seven years.

1 · Today

Siân Heder's Being Heumann, starring Ruth Madeley as the disability rights activist Judy Heumann, opens the 51st Toronto International Film Festival on 10 September, with Prima Facie and The Assassin(s) also set for Gala world premieres.

Variety, 7 Jul 2026
2 · The event

What happened

The Toronto International Film Festival's 51st edition runs from Thursday 10 September to Sunday 20 September. The opening night film is Siân Heder's Being Heumann, the story of the disability rights activist Judy Heumann and the 1977 sit-in she led, with Ruth Madeley, Mark Ruffalo, Dylan O'Brien and Rob Delaney (TIFF, Jul 2026) (Variety, 7 Jul 2026). It is Heder's first feature since CODA, which sold to Apple out of Sundance and won Best Picture (Deadline, Jul 2026).

The festival's other Gala world premieres include Susanna White's Prima Facie, with Cynthia Erivo, and Hur Jin-ho's The Assassin(s) (Variety, 7 Jul 2026). The People's Choice Award, voted by the audience across the programme, is announced on the last Sunday, 20 September.

The award matters because of what follows it. Deadline's record of past winners runs from Chariots of Fire to last year's Hamnet (Deadline), and the recent run is the cleanest bellwether in the autumn calendar.

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Seven People's Choice winners since 2019, and what the Academy did with each, taken from the Oscars' own ceremony records.

2025
2025: Chloé Zhao's Hamnet won the People's Choice Award and was nominated for Best Picture at the 98th Academy Awards, where Jessie Buckley won Best Actress (Academy, 2026).
2024
2024: Mike Flanagan's The Life of Chuck won the People's Choice Award and received no Best Picture nomination at the 97th Academy Awards (Academy, 2025).
2023
2023: Cord Jefferson's American Fiction won at Toronto and was nominated for Best Picture at the 96th Academy Awards, winning Adapted Screenplay (Academy, 2024).
2022
2022: Steven Spielberg's The Fabelmans won at Toronto and was nominated for Best Picture at the 95th Academy Awards (Academy, 2023).
2021
2021: Kenneth Branagh's Belfast won at Toronto and was nominated for Best Picture at the 94th Academy Awards (Academy, 2022).
2020
2020: Chloé Zhao's Nomadland won at Toronto and went on to win Best Picture at the 93rd Academy Awards (Academy, 2021).
2019
2019: Taika Waititi's Jojo Rabbit won at Toronto and was nominated for Best Picture at the 92nd Academy Awards, winning Adapted Screenplay (Academy, 2020).
6 of 7
In 6 of the last 7 years the TIFF People's Choice winner went on to a Best Picture nomination, and one, Nomadland, won. The miss, The Life of Chuck in 2024, was a small independent release with no studio campaign behind it. The audience ballot rewards crowd-pleasers, and the Academy's expanded Best Picture field has room for most of them.

Six Best Picture nominations in seven years, one winner, one miss.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is that the award goes to a film that arrives from Venice or Telluride with a reputation, which is how Nomadland, Jojo Rabbit and Hamnet won; the world-premiere market sits close to even because Toronto's own galas, The Fabelmans and American Fiction among them, have taken it just as often. Opening night films rarely place, which keeps the Being Heumann market low despite Heder's record.

The counter-case is the size of the lineup. With 293 films and an audience ballot, a well-received crowd-pleaser from the Gala programme can beat a festival-circuit favourite whose admirers have already seen it elsewhere. The Being Heumann prediction fails if the opening-night audience votes the way it did for CODA's story; the premiere market fails if the Venice winner, announced on 12 September, carries its momentum north.
