Nvidia04 Sep 2026

Nvidia guided $91bn and delivered $96bn. The next bar is $108bn.

Nvidia reported revenue of $96.2bn for the quarter to 26 July, up 106% on a year earlier, and told investors to expect $108bn next. In each of the last four quarters the company has finished above the top of its own forecast range.

4 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

Nvidia reported second-quarter revenue of $96.2 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from a year ago, with data-centre revenue of $89.0 billion, and guided third-quarter revenue to $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.

Nvidia, 26 Aug 2026
2 · The event

What happened

Nvidia's results for the quarter ended 26 July put revenue at $96.2bn, 18% above the prior quarter and 106% above a year earlier. Data-centre revenue was $89.0bn, up 117% year on year; gross margin was 75.0% on both GAAP and non-GAAP measures, and diluted earnings were $2.46 GAAP and $2.22 non-GAAP per share (Nvidia, 26 Aug 2026).

The outlook is the number the market trades. Nvidia expects third-quarter revenue of $108.0bn, plus or minus 2%, with gross margin of 74.0% and operating expenses of about $9.0bn non-GAAP, and it again assumes no data-centre compute revenue from China (Nvidia, 26 Aug 2026). The guidance implies another 12% sequential step on a base that has doubled in a year.

The company's own record is the context. Three months earlier it had guided the just-completed quarter to $91.0bn plus or minus 2% (Nvidia, 20 May 2026); it delivered $96.2bn, above the top of that range, as it has in every quarter of the current run.

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Four consecutive quarters, four outlooks, four results. Every number comes from Nvidia's own earnings releases.

2026
May–August 2026: the outlook was $91.0bn plus or minus 2% (Nvidia, 20 May 2026); revenue came in at $96.2bn, 3.7% above the top of the range (Nvidia, 26 Aug 2026).
2026
February–May 2026: the outlook was $78.0bn plus or minus 2% (Nvidia, 25 Feb 2026); revenue was $81.6bn, up 85% on a year earlier (Nvidia, 20 May 2026).
2025
November 2025–February 2026: the outlook was $65.0bn plus or minus 2% (Nvidia, 19 Nov 2025); revenue was $68.1bn, taking fiscal 2026 to $215.9bn (Nvidia, 25 Feb 2026).
2025
August–November 2025: the outlook was $54.0bn plus or minus 2% (Nvidia, 27 Aug 2025); revenue was $57.0bn, up 62% on a year earlier (Nvidia, 19 Nov 2025).
4 of 4
In 4 of the last 4 quarters Nvidia's revenue came in above the top of its own guidance range: $57.0bn against $54.0bn, $68.1bn against $65.0bn, $81.6bn against $78.0bn and $96.2bn against $91.0bn. The beats ranged from 3.5% to 5.6% above the midpoint. The pattern says the $108bn outlook is a floor, and the question for the stock is how much of that is already in the price.

Four outlooks, four beats of the top of the range, by 3.5% to 5.6%.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is that the company beats again; four for four on guidance is as clean as a dataset gets, and the outlook still excludes China entirely, which leaves upside if licences move. The price market is priced at even money because a beat is what the market already expects: the stock's reaction on 27 August came from guidance that exceeded forecasts, and the next earnings date is outside the window.

The honest counter-case is that guidance beats and share prices are different things. The stock fell after the May report despite a beat, and a fortnight of macro risk, from the ECB and Fed meetings to the Gulf, will move a $5 trillion company more than any datacentre order. The price prediction fails on a broad market fall; the China prediction fails if Washington's export licences stay where they have been all year.
