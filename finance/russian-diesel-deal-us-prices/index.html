US fuel prices11 Oct 2026

Trump let Russian diesel into the US. Will the first cargo arrive by 31 October?

Donald Trump said on 9 October that Russia will send diesel to the US. The Treasury paused sanctions on it until April. Americans vote on 3 November.

4 min read

What happened

Donald Trump announced a deal for Russia to release diesel to the US and world markets, and the US Treasury issued a temporary licence for Russian diesel.

Donald Trump said on 9 October that Russia will send diesel to the US. He said Vladimir Putin agreed to release 300,000 tonnes immediately, 500,000 tonnes in November and another million tonnes after that. The US Treasury paused sanctions on Russian diesel exports until 7 April.

The same day the Treasury issued a licence that allows Russian diesel to be sold, delivered and imported into the US. Trump wrote that diesel prices will be COMING DOWN, IN RECORD NUMBERS, AND FAST! He called lower prices for farmers and truck drivers his greatest priority.

All transactions … related to the sale, delivery, offloading, or importation, including importation into the United States, of diesel fuel of Russian Federation origin are authorized through 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time, April 7, 2027.

US Treasury, Office of Foreign Assets Control, General License No. 135, 9 October 2026

The average US diesel price was $6.28 a gallon on the day of the deal, according to the motoring group AAA, down from a record $6.53 at the end of September. Tim Armitage of the investment firm Quilter Cheviot said the first part is about 2.25 million barrels, while the US uses about 3.8 million barrels a day, so it won’t make a material difference to pump prices in the US.

Russia banned diesel exports earlier this year. Its diesel production has fallen by nearly 30% after Ukrainian drone attacks on its refineries, the International Energy Agency estimates. Putin said Russia is willing to supply the US market, but he gave no amounts.

Ukraine’s President Volodymyr Zelensky called the deal an investment in a war that must be ended, not prolonged. The Democratic leader in the Senate, Chuck Schumer, and two other Democratic senators called it a failure. Don Bacon, a Republican member of the House of Representatives from Nebraska, also criticised it. A Russia sanctions law that Trump signed last month gives him wide power to make exceptions.

Americans vote for Congress on 3 November. The House of Representatives does not hold votes again until 9 November.

Sources: BBC, US Treasury, The Guardian, House Majority Leader

The Pattern

We looked for cases where a government eased sanctions on oil or fuel from a rival or sanctioned country, and said it did so to bring prices down or keep supply flowing. We found eight such moves from 2018 to 2026, seven by the US and one by the UK. Each time we compared the weekly diesel price in the week of the move with the first weekly price four weeks later.

The Read

The price question splits evenly. Diesel was cheaper four weeks later after four of the eight earlier moves, and each time it fell, the first fall came within a week (US Energy Information Administration). Delivery is a different matter, because this licence is for imports into the US itself. We also looked at four times a government let in oil from a supplier it had banned before. The first cargo arrived within three weeks once, Iraqi oil in the US in December 1996, while Libyan oil in 2004, Iranian oil in Europe in 2016 and Venezuelan oil in 2023 took 39 to 68 days (US Energy Information Administration; US Energy Information Administration; Eurostat; US Energy Information Administration).

There are reasons for doubt. Seven of the eight price moves were made by the US government, and four came in one month, March 2026, during the war with Iran. The one fast delivery, Iraqi oil in 1996, came under a United Nations programme that set the first loading date, and no loading date for Russian diesel has been published. No lawmakers filed a bill or forced a vote against any of the eight moves within four weeks, and the House of Representatives does not vote again until 9 November. Russia’s refineries have been damaged, so it is unclear how much diesel it can send, including the 500,000 tonnes promised for November (United Nations; BBC).
