Build a new kind of media institution

We hire deliberately. Small team for now. Every role is shaped to ship something measurable in the first 90 days.

What we look for

  • Care about UK politics, public policy, or financial markets in your spare time
  • Read FT, Times, Telegraph, Guardian, or Economist with the same eyes as a critic
  • Ship things, not just plan them
  • Comfortable in a small founding team that uses AI heavily but is human-led
