Pro Ref14 Sep 2026

Referees admit derby goal was wrong. 7 of 8 such errors cost the official a game.

Pro Ref, the body that runs Premier League referees, said on 13 September Erling Haaland's derby winner at Manchester United should not have stood.

4 min read

What happened

Pro Ref, the Premier League refereeing body, said on the evening of 13 September that video assistant referee Matt Donohue made an error of judgement in allowing Erling Haaland's 60th-minute winner for Manchester City at Manchester United, that he should have recommended an on-field review, and that it had contacted United and would review the incident.

(BBC Sport 13 Sep)

Manchester City beat Manchester United 1-0 at Old Trafford on Sunday 13 September with ten men, Phil Foden having been sent off in the 23rd minute. Erling Haaland's goal on the hour was flagged offside on the pitch, then given after the video assistant referee, Matt Donohue, found Haaland onside and decided that Enzo Fernández, who was offside and dived at the ball, had not touched it. The same evening Pro Ref said the VAR had not recognised the likely impact of Fernández's position, should have sent the referee to the monitor, and had contacted United to acknowledge an error of judgement. (Guardian 13 Sep 2026; BBC Sport 13 Sep 2026; Independent 13 Sep 2026)

Pro Ref is the new name of the Professional Game Match Officials body, formerly PGMOL and then PGMO, which appoints and manages Premier League referees and VARs. Donohue has refereed only four Premier League games but was the VAR for 24 league matches last season. Sky Sports reported that United received an apology, and on 14 September Arsenal said they would also contact Pro Ref for an explanation of the penalty given against Ezri Konsa at Sunderland the day before. (Sky Sports 14 Sep 2026; Sky Sports 14 Sep 2026; BBC Sport 14 Sep 2026)

The admission lands on a club already under strain. United have four points from four games, equal to their worst start to a Premier League season since 1992-93, and thousands of fans protested against the owners before the derby. Their next match is at Fulham on Sunday 20 September, the last game of Matchweek 5, which runs from Friday 18 to Sunday 20 September. Pro Ref's officials list for Matchweek 4 went up on premierleague.com on Monday 7 September, six days before the round, so the Matchweek 5 list is due within days. (BBC Sport 13 Sep 2026; Premier League 7 Jul 2026; Premier League 7 Sep 2026)

The Read

Pro Ref admits fast because it has built a policy of doing so. Since Howard Webb took charge in December 2022 the body has treated a same-week apology and a missed round as the price of a clear error, and the BBC reported in 2023 that non-selection after the Wolves penalty was a direct result of his demand for accountability. The Key Match Incidents panel, which includes a Pro Ref representative, would log the Haaland goal anyway, so owning it on the night costs less than a week of argument. United's need is an apology they can show their supporters; Arsenal are now in the same queue. Players with a move before these markets close: Pro Ref, which publishes the Matchweek 5 list this week and controls the audio; United's board, with Fulham on 20 September and accounts on 23 September; and the panel, whose verdict follows. (BBC Sport 14 Aug 2023; BBC Sport 13 Sep 2026; BBC Sport 14 Sep 2026)

The weighted case is that Donohue's name is missing from the Matchweek 5 list. Seven of eight named officials missed the next round, every one of the six under Webb, and in the two most recent cases Salisbury was pulled within three days. Pro Ref's statement named the VAR's failure to recommend a review, which is the same wording that preceded Salisbury's removal in August 2025, so the first market opens at 78, a little under the base rate. The return market sits at 25 because the body kept Darren England off Liverpool games for the rest of 2023-24 after the Díaz error, and United have only about eleven league games before 13 December. (BBC Sport 31 Aug 2025; Guardian 1 Oct 2023; Telegraph 4 Oct 2023)

The honest counter-case is that the record measures Webb's regime as much as the error. The one miss came under Mike Riley, and Pro Ref's statement called the Fernández offence subjective, softer language than the significant human error of 2023. Donohue is also a specialist VAR, one of the few who worked more than twenty league games in the role last season, and in May the body kept him at VAR for the final round when the named fault was Salisbury's. The first market fails if the Matchweek 5 list carries his name in any role; the audio market fails if Pro Ref explains through the panel rather than on air; the Carrick market fails if a defeat at Fulham moves the board before the October break. (Sky Sports 14 Sep 2026; Premier League 20 May 2026; Sky Sports 14 Sep 2026)
