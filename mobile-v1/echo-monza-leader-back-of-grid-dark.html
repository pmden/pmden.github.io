Formula 104 Sep 2026

Antonelli leads by 59 and starts at the back. The championship leader has won Monza twice in seven years.

Kimi Antonelli takes a new power unit and a back-of-grid start at his home race on 6 September, a week after Lando Norris won a second straight race at Zandvoort. Since 2019 the driver leading the championship has won the Italian Grand Prix twice, both times Max Verstappen.

3 min readEcho #116INTERNATIONAL
1 · Today

Championship leader Kimi Antonelli will take a full new power unit and start the Italian Grand Prix from the back of the grid, Mercedes confirmed, with Toto Wolff calling Monza the best track to take the penalty; Antonelli leads George Russell and Lewis Hamilton by 59 points.

Formula 1, 31 Aug 2026
2 · The event

What happened

Mercedes confirmed that Kimi Antonelli will take a complete new power unit at Monza and start from the back of the grid on Sunday 6 September. Toto Wolff said the team's calculations made it the best track to take the penalty. Antonelli leads the championship by 59 points from both George Russell and Lewis Hamilton, and Ferrari, joint second through Hamilton, have been Mercedes's closest challenger (Formula 1, 31 Aug 2026).

The form is with McLaren. Lando Norris won the Dutch Grand Prix on 23 August from Antonelli and Russell, his second consecutive victory, after the race was red-flagged on the opening lap when Max Verstappen crashed heavily at the banked final corner (FIA, 23 Aug 2026).

Monza is a track that has not rewarded the points leader. In each of the last seven years the leader arrived with an advantage and in five of them someone else won, including a rookie's first victory in 2020 and Ferrari's first home win in nine years in 2019.

3 · The pattern

The pattern

Seven Italian Grands Prix from 2019 to 2025: who led the championship on arrival, and who won. Results from Formula 1's own archive.

2025
September 2025: Oscar Piastri arrived 34 points clear of Norris; Verstappen won from Norris and Piastri (Formula 1, 7 Sep 2025) (Sky Sports, 7 Sep 2025).
2024
September 2024: Verstappen led the standings; Charles Leclerc won for Ferrari (Formula 1, 2024 results).
2023
September 2023: Verstappen, leading the championship, won a record tenth consecutive race (Formula 1, 2023 results).
2022
September 2022: Verstappen, leading the championship, won behind the safety car (Formula 1, 2022 results).
2021
September 2021: Verstappen led Hamilton by three points; the two collided and Daniel Ricciardo won for McLaren (Formula 1, 2021 results).
2020
September 2020: Hamilton led the championship; Pierre Gasly took his first win for AlphaTauri (Formula 1, 2020 results).
2019
September 2019: Hamilton led the championship; Leclerc held off both Mercedes for Ferrari's first Monza win since 2010 (Formula 1, 8 Sep 2019).
2 of 7
In 2 of the last 7 Italian Grands Prix the championship leader won: Verstappen in 2022 and 2023, in the two seasons Red Bull dominated. In the other five the leader was beaten by a Ferrari at home, a McLaren, an AlphaTauri and, last year, by Verstappen from behind. This year the leader starts last.

Two wins for the points leader in seven, both from pole-position form Verstappen no longer has.

5 · The read

How to read it

The weighted case is that the leader does not win, and this year the base rate has help: starting last at a track where the leader has lost five of seven from the front. That is why the Antonelli market is priced near 10%. The Mercedes market is a different question, because Russell starts from wherever the car qualifies and the car has led the season.

The honest counter-case is the car itself. Antonelli's 59-point lead is the largest at this stage in years, Monza's long straights reward the fastest package regardless of grid slot, and the 2023 precedent shows a dominant car can win here in any circumstances. The Norris prediction fails if McLaren's two-race run was track-specific; the Mercedes prediction fails if Ferrari, at home and with Hamilton joint second, takes the race the way Leclerc did in 2019 and 2024.
