Solheim Cup04 Sep 2026

Europe hosts at Bernardus. The home side has won four of the last eight Solheim Cups.

Anna Nordqvist's Europe meets Angela Stanford's United States at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands from 11 to 13 September, with the Americans defending.

3 min read

What happened

Angela Stanford completed the United States' Solheim Cup team with Megan Khang, Rose Zhang and Lindy Duncan as captain's picks for the 11–13 September match at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands, where Europe's side under Anna Nordqvist was finalised earlier in August.

(Sky Sports, 26 Aug 2026)

The United States named their team on 26 August: Nelly Korda, Angel Yin, Jennifer Kupcho, Auston Kim, Yealimi Noh, Lauren Coughlin, Allisen Corpuz, Andrea Lee and Alison Lee qualified, and Angela Stanford added Megan Khang, Rose Zhang and Lindy Duncan, a 35-year-old debutant, as picks (Sky Sports, 26 Aug 2026). Zhang led the US with four points from four matches in 2024.

Europe's captain Anna Nordqvist had already completed her side with Leona Maguire, who has 8.5 points from 12 Solheim matches, and three rookies, Julia Lopez Ramirez, Nastasia Nadaud and Mimi Rhodes, joining Charley Hull, Lottie Woad, Esther Henseleit, Maja Stark, Celine Boutier, Carlota Ciganda, Linn Grant and Nanna Koerstz Madsen (Sky Sports, Aug 2026).

The match is at Bernardus Golf near Cromvoirt, the first Solheim Cup in the Netherlands. The Americans hold the cup after a 15.5–12.5 win at Robert Trent Jones Golf Club in 2024, their first since 2017 (Sky Sports, 15 Sep 2024).

The Pattern

Eight Solheim Cups from 2011 to 2024, taken from the event's own record book and the 2024 result: where each was played, and whether the home side won.

2024
September 2024: the United States beat Europe 15.5–12.5 at Robert Trent Jones, Lilia Vu's half-point at the last securing the cup (Sky Sports, 15 Sep 2024).
2023
September 2023: Europe and the United States tied 14–14 at Finca Cortesin, the first tie in the event's history, and Europe kept the cup as holders (Solheim Cup record book).
2021
September 2021: Europe won 15–13 at Inverness Club in Toledo, an away victory (Solheim Cup record book).
2019
September 2019: Europe won 14.5–13.5 at Gleneagles on the final putt of the final singles (Solheim Cup record book).
2017
August 2017: the United States won 16.5–11.5 at Des Moines (Solheim Cup record book).
2015
September 2015: the United States came from 10–6 down to win 14.5–13.5 at St Leon-Rot (Solheim Cup record book).
2013
August 2013: Europe won 18–10 at Colorado Golf Club, its first victory on American soil (Solheim Cup record book).
2011
September 2011: Europe won 15–13 at Killeen Castle (Solheim Cup record book).
4 of 8
In 4 of the last 8 Solheim Cups the home team won outright: 2011, 2017, 2019 and 2024. The away team won in 2013, 2015 and 2021, and 2023 was tied with Europe retaining as holders. Home advantage in this event is real but no better than a coin toss, and the United States have won three of the last four meetings on either side of the Atlantic.

Four home wins, three away wins and a tie in eight.

The Read

The weighted case is a close match decided on Sunday, which is what six of the last eight have been; the home market sits just under even because the base rate is four in eight and the Americans arrive with the stronger recent record and the world No. 1. Europe's edge is course knowledge and a team built around two players, Hull and Woad, who have won on this side of the Atlantic all summer.

The honest counter-case is 2013 and 2021, when Europe won in America by margins that had nothing to do with the venue, and 2017, when the Americans won at home by five. The margin market is priced low because only two of the eight were decided by four or more; the Europe prediction fails if the three European rookies are exposed in the foursomes, where the 2024 match was lost.
